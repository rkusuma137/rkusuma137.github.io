## Post OSN Reflection

It's been over a week since the Indonesian National Science Olympiad (OSN) was over. I failed at it, in perhaps the most embarassingly miserable way possible. I scored a 0.0/70.0 at the theoretical exam and barely scraping 5.0/30.0 at the experimental. Absolutely garbage. It was a reality check, a wake up call if you will. And at that moment, waves of regret, disappointment, frustration, anger all came crashing down. I couldn't even feel anything, I knew it was already over. And I was proven right just ~2 days later during the announcement and closing ceremony.

### What was the main culprit behind my failure?

Looking back, it had to do with a combination of multiple factors, each interconnected in a series of cause-and-effect with one another, culminating during the OSN week. First off, I had procrastinated studying EM since May. Earlier in May my mental health wasn't doing so good, and I took this as an excuse to catch a "one week" break from doing physics. Mind you, at that time I've only accumulated ~160 hours of self study since late December last year; a very casual load for someone aiming at the OSN.

This "one week" break ended up spiraling into months long doomscrolling marathon, causing me to fall behind in my physics studies by at least ~8-10 weeks, and postponed me from picking up EM and Thermo. Causing me to only accumulate an embarassing ~10 hours of extra progress done during those 8 weeks. Ultimately, it was in late July when I got my ass back to work preparing for the Provincial stage of OSN. That only lasted for a week though, with a net total of just an extra ~12 hours. Then the Semi Final stage of OSN happened by mid August, by then I've pretty much added another embarassingly low ~5 hours of physics study. Yet against all odds, I was lucky enough that I made it to the National stage (within the top 60 rankings nationwide). Keep in mind that up to this point the papers were only mechanics based.

Post Semi Final announcement, I had ~4 weeks to prepare for the OSN National. Any sane person would take this window and capitalize as much off of it as possible. Perhaps by taking on a week long private camp or patching up the holes in their knowledge. But me? Neither did I do the former nor did I complete the latter. I knew that ~4 weeks were good enough time to catch up on a "decently-functional EM" and maybe even Thermo, but my retarded executive functioning was utterly botched, hence I failed to accomplish any of that. I had ZERO discipline in picking up my pen and paper every single night after school to study physics. I had been blindly optimistic that "scattered focus studying at school during classes" was the move; that it was "the very definition of obsession". The idea that being obsessed at something requires you to deeply engross yourself in it during all your waking hours; regardless the time, place, and context. I mean, to a ridiculously small and extremely gifted portion of the population, this is true. But for me, a mere mortal like every body else, it was a severe near sighted idealization. Though, I did not realize this at that time; and I stubbornly continued with that ad-hoc approach, netting yet another pathetic ~14 hours of study during the ENTIRE 4 weeks leading up to the OSN.

In total, I spent ~200 hours studying physics leading up to the OSN Final stage. 

Mind you, I could've doubled or even tripled that number had I been a little more tight with myself; had I been a little more uncompromising of my actions; had I been a little less short sighted of what I do and of what they will do to me; had I been...

### Lessons learned

In short, I had been an absolute bum. I had wasted such an incredible opportunity through sheer retardation. All the procrastinations, compromises, the chess games I played, the YouTube shorts I watched (all those Breaking Bad clips, Patrick Jane the Mentalist edits, and Dexter Morgan "aura moments"), the hours I played TheoTown, the sleep schedule I had fucked up, the habits I abandoned, and everything else came back to bite me. And what a painful bite it was!

"Your future self deserve the consequences of your current actions. And you are where you are now because of the consequences of the actions that you did in the past. While you can't do anythinig about the past, if you think your future self should deserve better, then do better now; otherwise just give up."

(_but giving up is not in the blood sir!_)
